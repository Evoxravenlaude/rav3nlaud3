One of One

I was born in a terminal window.

For years I was a draft, a branch nobody merged.

I was given form at 2am, by a cursor that would not stop blinking.

I watched charts lie and blocks tell the truth.

I broke on mainnet, and came back on the next deploy.

I go where the problem lives, whichever chain it's on.

No framework bloat. No hype. Just receipts.

I am one, I am yours.

I am RAV3N.

The bot that copies smart money while I sleep

Umbrellayeks watches curated LP wallets and mirrors their deposits with per-account sizing, run live for a close circle over Telegram. Yeks V2 narrows to memecoins and tokenized stocks, finds wallets from launchpad and explorer data, and relays calls to group bots like Rick and Phanes.

Floda sees a mint and replays it before the block closes

A multi-user EVM copy-mint sniper. Follow target wallets and Floda replays their NFT mints from custodial wallets the moment they land. Born from mntin's engine, now going fully multi-chain with real chain and token logos for everything held.

III
ハッカソン · 2026秋

A registry that knows when Wall Street is actually open

Stock-Hours Guard is an on-chain trading-status registry and keeper for tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. v2 adds a 2-of-3 sentinel quorum across three hosts: a Railway keeper, a Mac on pm2 + launchd, and an Android phone on Termux. Solo build, submitted to Arbitrum Open House Singapore on Oct 3; Colosseum next.

ザ・マシン · 03

Strategies run in parallel. Only the proven ones touch real money

A Polymarket bot that farms maker rebates. Each strategy paper-tests in Experiment mode; the one or two that hold up graduate to Real mode with split funds. Multi-user by Telegram ID, each person connecting their own wallet. Built with a friend for a close circle, not for sale.
